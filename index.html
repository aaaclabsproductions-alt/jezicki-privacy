Privacy Policy

Jezički Asistent

Effective date: March 17, 2026

Overview

Jezički Asistent ("the App") is a multilingual translation and language learning tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the App works.

Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No names, email addresses, or contact information collected
  • No location data collected
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs collected
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No cookies are used

Translation Requests

When you enter a word or phrase for translation, that text is sent to a server to generate translations. These requests are processed in real time and are not stored, logged, or associated with any user identity. No translation history is retained on our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App uses a third-party AI service (Anthropic) to generate translations. Translation text is sent to this service for processing. Anthropic's use of data is governed by their own privacy policy. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

Data Storage

The App may store your learning progress (such as flashcard state or quiz scores) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal information from any user, it is safe for use by all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
